A LITTLE CONTEXT

Why the median?

The middle sale price gives a useful sense of a typical transaction, with less influence from very expensive sales than an average.

These are observed sales, not a valuation of every home or a measure of today's listings.

Full data description ↗

One price rule

Only finite sale prices of at least $1,000 are included. The same rule applies to every summary, bar and table row.

Source and excluded-record counts are calculated from the dataset.

One sale, one record

Records are identified by listing_id. Repeated street addresses are kept: they may represent separate apartments or repeat sales. Cities are grouped with their state.

A median of sales

For each view, the middle price is calculated from all eligible transactions. For an even count, the two middle prices are averaged. City medians are never averaged to create the overall figure.

Limits of this snapshot

Prices above $5 million remain included. Home type is unreliable; this page does not use it. It also does not use incomplete dates or zero floor areas to calculate market duration or price per square foot. Values are source-reported and not verified against county records.
